## Source quality

PACETEQ gives the most race-pace numbers but never shows its lap list, stint filters or code. Motor Sport Magazine has the clearest qualifying method but covers half a season. RacingNews365 disagrees with PACETEQ on VER-PER and ALO-STR 2023. Formula1.com and ESPN give no seconds per lap.
